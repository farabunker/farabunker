# farabunker — Brand & Name

## The name

**farabunker** — a coined blend of **Faraday** (as in the *Faraday cage*, the enclosure
that blocks all electromagnetic signals — the perfect metaphor for "sealed off the network")
and **bunker** (a fortified, self-contained shelter). Together: *a sealed, secure shelter
for your AI and data.*

Pronounced "fair-uh-BUNK-er."

## Why this name

The project was originally conceived as **"DigitalBunker,"** but that name was already taken
for both the domain and the YouTube channel. Naming criteria for the replacement:

- **Easy to spell and pronounce.**
- **Coined / distinctive**, so it can be trademarked and can own its search results — a
  plain descriptive name (e.g. "OfflineAI") is both taken and unregistrable.
- **On-concept** — evokes the offline, sealed, secure idea.
- **Available** as a domain and across social handles.

Candidates considered included coined words (Autark, Redoubt, Faradai), Zillow-style
invented words (Vozzo, Zollo), and the literal "FaradayBunker." The literal form was
rejected because "Faraday" sits in a **crowded trademark field** (Faraday Technology, Faraday
Future, Faraday Inc.) and is buried in search by Faraday cages / Michael Faraday, and because
"Faraday Bunker" already exists as a music release. **Farabunk** was a strong coined
candidate but its "-bunk" ending carries the *"that's bunk / debunk = nonsense"* idiom —
the opposite of a trust signal. **farabunker** resolves that: "-bunker" completes into a
positive, on-theme word while staying coined and distinctive.
