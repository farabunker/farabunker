# Licensing

farabunker uses a **dual-licensing model** (see [docs/BUSINESS.md](docs/BUSINESS.md) and
[docs/adr/0003-dual-licensing-and-cla.md](docs/adr/0003-dual-licensing-and-cla.md)).

## Open-source license

The farabunker source is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0**
(AGPL-3.0) — the full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

In plain terms, AGPL-3.0 means you may use, study, modify, and share farabunker freely,
**provided that** if you distribute it *or run a modified version as a network service*, you
make your corresponding source available under the same license. Individuals, hobbyists,
homelabbers, and off-grid users are fully served by this license at no cost, forever.

AGPL-3.0 is **decided**, not provisional: the copyleft text was ratified on 2026-09-20 during
release preparation, and [docs/adr/0002-license-direction.md](docs/adr/0002-license-direction.md)
is marked Accepted. AGPL-3.0 + dual licensing is the model farabunker ships under.

## Commercial license ("closing the loop")

farabunker is owned by **pc analytics LLC**, which holds the copyright and grants both
licenses. If you want to embed farabunker in a **closed-source** product, or run it as a
service **without** publishing your modifications, you can purchase a commercial license
from pc analytics LLC that waives the AGPL copyleft obligations. *Closing the loop is
possible — you pay to support the project.* Commercial licensing is not yet
operational; contact details will be added once the business entity is set up.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under a Contributor License Agreement granting pc analytics LLC
the rights it needs to offer both the open-source and commercial licenses above. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CLA.md](CLA.md).

**The CLA remains under legal review.** Until it is finalized, external contributions are
held rather than merged — there is no agreement in place yet for the project to accept them
under. Maintainer commits are covered by ownership and are unaffected.
