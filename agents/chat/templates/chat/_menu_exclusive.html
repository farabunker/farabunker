A four-line listener is smaller and says what it means.) ONE DELEGATED LISTENER ON THE RAIL, never one per row: the rail's rows are re-rendered on every page load and there is no client-side row insertion anywhere on this surface, but a listener per row would still be N listeners where one does the job, and it would have to be re-bound by anything that ever did insert a row. CAPTURE PHASE, AND THAT IS LOAD-BEARING. `toggle` does NOT bubble (`Event.bubbles` is false on it), so a listener on the rail would never see a row's own toggle in the bubble phase at all. Capture propagates to ancestors regardless of `bubbles`, which is the same reason the platform recommends it for `focus`/`load` delegation. The third argument to `addEventListener` below is that `true`. THE `chat-menu` GUARD IS ALSO LOAD-BEARING: the menu panel contains NESTED `
` of its own (Rename, and the Delete confirm), and each of those fires its own `toggle` through this same listener. Without the class check, opening Rename would close the very menu it lives inside. The `open` check keeps a CLOSING menu from re-entering the loop that its own `other.open = false` writes trigger. DISMISSAL IS THE OTHER HALF OF "LIKE A DROP DOWN" (fix round 1). Mutual exclusion alone left a menu open until you clicked its own summary again; a real dropdown also closes when you click away from it and when you press Escape. Both are folded into THIS script rather than a second one, and both reuse the same `closeMenus` the toggle handler uses: * `pointerdown` ON THE DOCUMENT, not `click`, and in the CAPTURE phase: it fires before the click reaches whatever was clicked, so a menu closing never swallows or re-orders the interaction that closed it. `pointerdown` also covers touch and pen, which a `mousedown` listener would miss. `event.target.closest("details.chat-menu")` is what keeps a click INSIDE an open panel -- on Rename, on a form field, on Save -- from closing the menu out from under itself; only a click whose target has no `.chat-menu` ancestor dismisses. * `keydown` ON THE DOCUMENT for `Escape`, which is where a keyboard user expects it and which works whether focus is on the summary, inside the Rename field, or nowhere in particular. THE `closest` GUARD IS DOCUMENT-WIDE, NOT RAIL-SCOPED, and that has a second effect worth naming: clicking ANY `details.chat-menu` on the page spares an open rail menu, including `chat/workstream_settings.html`'s "Manage" disclosure, which reuses this same class deliberately OUTSIDE the rail. So opening Manage does not dismiss an open rail menu. Left as it is on purpose -- it is the friendlier reading (two menus the operator deliberately opened, neither yanked away), and narrowing the guard to `rail.contains(inside)` would buy a tidier invariant nobody asked for at the cost of a surprise. A MENU CLOSES ALL THE WAY DOWN. `closeMenus` clears every nested `
` inside a menu before closing the menu itself -- the Rename form and the Delete confirm keep their OWN `open` attribute otherwise, so a row whose Rename you had expanded would reopen days later still showing a stale, pre-filled form the operator never asked for. Invisible while the parent is shut, which is exactly why it has to be cleared on the way out rather than noticed on the way back in. Clearing them fires their own `toggle` events, which the handler above returns from immediately (they are not `.chat-menu`, and they are closing, not opening) -- no re-entrancy. NOTHING ELSE IS ADDED: no focus trapping, no roving tabindex, no click-outside-to-focus-restore. The menu is a native `

PROGRESSIVE ENHANCEMENT, exactly like `chat/_enter_to_send.html`: with JavaScript off every menu still opens and closes natively -- two of them can simply be open at once and a click elsewhere leaves them open, which is the untidy-but-not-broken behaviour this surface shipped with before this round.
